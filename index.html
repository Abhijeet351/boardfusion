Marble Loop IN DEVELOPMENT

Our original Jackaroo-style mode: 2 or 4 players race marbles from their base around the loop and into their safe zone using a hand of playing cards instead of dice.

  • Cards as moves: number cards move that many spaces; A starts a marble or moves 1; K starts a marble or moves 13; Q moves 12; J swaps one of your marbles with any marble on the loop; 4 moves backward; 7 splits between two marbles.
  • Teams: 4-player games are 2v2 - partners sit opposite and win together.
  • Captures: landing on an opponent's marble sends it back to base.

The rules engine, card deck, and board renderer are designed in the README. The playable build ships in the next milestone; Dice Race is fully playable today.

Dice Race

Original Ludo-style race for local or server-verified online play.

Roll a 6 to leave base. Landing on an opponent sends them home; star squares are safe. A 6, a capture, or getting a token home earns another roll - but three 6s in a row ends your turn. First to bring all 4 tokens home wins.
