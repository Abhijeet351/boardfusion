Marble Loop

Original Jackaroo-style card race. 2 players duel; 4 players form 2v2 teams (partners sit opposite). Pass-and-play on one device.

Play one card per turn. A or K brings a marble out of base. Number cards move that far; 4 moves backward; 7 can split between two marbles; J swaps one of your marbles with any marble on the loop; Q moves 12. Land on an opponent to send them home. Bring all your marbles into your colored lane to win (exact count needed for the last step). No legal move? Discard a card and pass.
