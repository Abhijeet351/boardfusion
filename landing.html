Two classic board games, one room code. Play with friends anywhere, or pass one phone around.

Play now - no install Create an online room

Dice Race

The dice classic you already know. Roll a 6 to break out, capture rivals, race all four tokens home. 2-4 players, solo against the computer, pass-and-play, or online rooms.

Marble Loop

Cards instead of dice. Split a 7 between two marbles, swap with a Jack, and back up with a 4. Duel 1v1 or team up 2v2.

  • No accounts, no downloads - opens in any browser
  • Online rooms - share a 4-letter code and play in real time
  • Pass-and-play + computer seats for couch games and solo practice
  • Made for phones - touch-first, works on desktop too
BoardFusion is an original, independent project. Dice Race and Marble Loop are our own takes on classic public-domain board game formats.
